Q: What is the attendance policy?
A: - Attending class is mandatory.
- Attendance is recorded from the first day of class, regardless of when registration is completed.
- If a student's number of absences exceeds one-third of the total number of classes for a given course, it may lead to consequences.
--------------------------------------------------

Q: What happens if I miss a class?
A: - If a student misses a class, they will receive class handouts and instructions on self-study content from the course instructor.
- By supplementing their studies with the provided materials, they will not be considered absent.
- Excused absences can only be applied to a maximum of less than half of the total number of classes for each course.
- If a student's number of absences exceeds one-third of the total number of classes for a given course, they may lose eligibility to take exams and receive an “F” or “NP” for the course.
--------------------------------------------------

Q: How are grades calculated?
A: - GPA indicates the average grade points earned per credit.
- It is calculated using a specific formula (not provided in detail).
- Grades are expressed to the second decimal place with the third rounded up.
- If a student retakes a failed course, the failing grade is not counted toward total registered credits; only the final grade is counted.
- Grades for GPA calculation include A (4.0), B (3.0), C (2.0), D (1.0), and F (0.0).
- Grades P and NP are not counted in GPA calculation.
--------------------------------------------------

Q: What is the policy on repeating a course?
A: - Students cannot re-register for courses in which they earned credit.
- Students can re-register for courses they failed.
- If a student retakes a failed course, the failing grade will not be counted toward the total number of registered credits.
- Only the final grade from the retake will be counted.
--------------------------------------------------

Q: What are the prerequisites for advanced courses?
A: - Course prerequisites are conditions that must be met prior to enrolling in a course.
- Prerequisites can include:
  - Completion of a specific course.
  - Meeting certain standards in the Program, Foundation Courses, and specific courses from each subject area.
- For certain courses, students must:
  - Have a GPA of 2.5 or higher at the time of registering for ‘Seminar’.
  - Receive necessary approval from an advisor.
  - Attend relevant information session(s).
- Students can apply for a Prerequisite Waiver during the course registration period if they do not meet a prerequisite but have equivalent knowledge.

Q: What happens if I miss an exam?
A: - Consult with the course instructor if you miss an exam.
- Students eligible for a make-up exam must follow the necessary procedures outlined in the iCLA Navi.
- If you miss an exam and received an “F” or “NP” due to attendance, you are not eligible for a re-take exam.
- The highest grade possible for a re-take exam is a “D” or “P”.
- Results of re-take exams are notified on UNIPA on the day of grade release.
--------------------------------------------------

Q: What is the policy on make-up exams?
A: - A make-up exam is offered when a student cannot take the scheduled final exam due to unavoidable circumstances.
- Evaluation of the make-up exam includes:
  - Cooperative preparation of answers by multiple people.
  - Plagiarism of other examinees' answers.
  - Malicious acts such as using cheat sheets, cheating, or private conversations.
  - Writing answers on allowed references and lending or borrowing references.
- Students must provide a document proving the reason for not being able to take the exam.
--------------------------------------------------

- Specific actions that are prohibited include:
  - Cooperative preparation of answers by multiple people.

  - Malicious acts such as using cheat sheets, cheating, and private conversations.

Q: Can international students work part-time?
A: - Yes, international students are permitted to work part-time while studying in Japan.
- They can work up to 28 hours per week during the semester.
- During long breaks, they may work part-time up to 8 hours per day.
- Specific long breaks include:
  - Winter Break: December 25, 2025 - January 6, 2026
  - Spring Break: March 16, 2026 - March 24, 2026
  - Summer Break: August 4, 2025 - August 26, 2025
- Students can apply for permission to work more than 28 hours per week outside of long breaks.
- It is illegal to only attend a part-time job without attending classes, which can lead to deportation.
