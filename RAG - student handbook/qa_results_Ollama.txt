Q: What happens if I miss a class?
A:  If you miss a class due to unavoidable circumstances:
- You can apply for an "Excused Absence".
- If approved, the course instructor will provide you with class handouts and instructions on self-study content.
- By supplementing your studies to the same extent as the missed class with the provided materials, you will not receive an "F" or "NP" grade for that class.
- Excused Absences can only be applied to a maximum of less than half of the total number of classes for each course.
- If your absence exceeds one-third of the total number of classes for a given course, your graduation may be postponed or your year level and semester will remain the same but a Leave of Absence counts towards a student’s Maximum Enrollment Period.
- If you miss an exam due to unavoidable circumstances, you should consult with the course instructor about taking a make-up exam.

If you are late to class due to disruptions in public transportation:
- You should contact the iCLA Administrative Office regarding the status of the class if your instructor does not show up at least 25 minutes after the class start time. The course instructor decides how arriving late is handled.

Q: How are grades calculated?
A:  - Grades are calculated based on a five-grade scale, with letter grades and their Grade Points (GP) as follows:
    - A: 100-90, GP: 4.0, Interpretation of grade: Excellent
    - B: 89-80, GP: 3.0, Interpretation of grade: Good
    - C: 79-70, GP: 2.0, Interpretation of grade: Satisfactory
    - D: 69-60, GP: 1.0, Interpretation of grade: Marginal
    - F: 59-0, GP: 0.0, Interpretation of grade: Fail
- If a student retakes a failed course, only the final grade will be counted for GPA calculation.
- Credits for each course and credits transferred from exchange partners are calculated according to the number of hours required to acquire the contents of a course, with 1 credit equal to 45 hours of study.
- Exams include final exams, exams given during class hours, and external exams related to a class subject. The passing grade for exams is dependent on the grade evaluation.
- Grades are posted on UNIPA in August for the spring semester and January for the fall semester.
- If a student suspects an error in grade evaluation or holds doubt about the evaluation of a course, they can appeal the grade.

- Re-enrollment in the University is possible with the President's permission after being dismissed or withdrawing for up to 5 years.
